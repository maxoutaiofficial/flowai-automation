Help

  1. Use Copy prompt on Setup, paste it into your coding agent, and let it finish.
  2. Open flow.google.com and stay signed in.
  3. Keep that tab visible. A covered or minimised window stalls jobs.
  4. The bridge listens on 127.0.0.1:8791 after the CLI from the prompt is installed.
